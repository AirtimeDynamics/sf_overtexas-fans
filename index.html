Season schedule

Opening hours.

Six Flags Over Texas is open year-round on select days. Hours change by date, so the official calendar is the final reference.

Fright Fest • Fridays

5 PM – 11 PM

In October 2026, Fridays ran from 5 p.m. to 11 p.m.; haunted houses open at 6 p.m.

Fright Fest • Sat & Sun

Sat 12–11 PM • Sun 12–10 PM

Weekend days open at noon, while most October weekdays are closed. Fright Fest runs through November 1.

Holiday in the Park

Late Nov – Dec

A holiday tradition since 1985; its 40th season began on November 23, 2025. Check the official calendar for 2026 dates.

Visit strategy

Plan it right.

One section combining the three practical things visitors usually need: when to come, how to plan a family day, and what to pack.

Best time to visit

Use the official calendar to pick a day, then check again for event hours such as Fright Fest and Holiday in the Park.

Outside summer, the park usually opens only on selected days, mostly weekends.
Arrive before opening and start at the far end of the park instead of the front.
Fright Fest and Holiday in the Park change the rhythm of the day.

Family planning

Start with rides that match your group’s height limits, then work outward area by area instead of crossing the park repeatedly.

Check the height requirement of major rides before queueing.
Bugs Bunny Boomtown and Boomtown’s family coasters are built for younger riders.
Keep meal, rest and shade breaks in the plan instead of treating them as an afterthought.

What to bring

Pack for a long outdoor day in the Texas sun, with heat, storms and changing weather.

Comfortable shoes and clothes you can move in all day.
Sun protection, a refillable water bottle and a hat for hot afternoons.
The park is cashless: bring a card or phone payment.
How it grew

65 years of
Texas thrills.

From the first Six Flags park in 1961 to the world’s first giga dive coaster: the milestones that shaped Six Flags Over Texas.

1961
1961Grand opening

The first Six Flags park

Angus G. Wynne Jr. opens the park on August 5 with sections for the six nations whose flags have flown over Texas.

1963
1963Log flume

El Aserradero

El Aserradero opens as Arrow Development’s first log flume.

1966
1966First mine train

Runaway Mine Train

Arrow’s first mine train coaster opens in Boomtown and still runs today.

1969
1969Landmark

The Oil Derrick

The 300 ft Oil Derrick observation tower rises and gives the Tower section its name.

1978
1978Double loop

Shock Wave

Shock Wave briefly becomes the world’s tallest roller coaster. It has been closed since March 2026.

1980
1980Wooden classic

Judge Roy Scream

The park’s only all-wood coaster opens, named after the frontier judge Roy Bean.

1990
1990Wood giant

Texas Giant

The Texas Giant opens as the world’s tallest wooden coaster and wins Golden Ticket awards in 1998 and 1999.

1998
1998Launch

Mr. Freeze

Mr. Freeze arrives with a launch from 0 to 70 mph in 3.8 seconds.

1999
1999Gotham City

Batman: The Ride

Gotham City opens with the B&M inverted coaster Batman: The Ride.

2001
2001Mega coaster

Titan

Titan opens with a 255 ft drop, and Wile E. Coyote’s Grand Canyon Blaster arrives for younger riders.

2011
201150th anniversary

New Texas Giant

RMC rebuilds the Texas Giant as the first I-Box hybrid, winning the Golden Ticket for Best New Ride of 2011.

2017
2017Free spin

The Joker

The Joker opens; in August the park replaces its six historical flags with American flags.

2023
2023Water coaster

Aquaman: Power Wave

The Mack PowerSplash launches riders up 150 ft towers into a splashdown.

2026
2026Giga dive

Tormenta Rampaging Run

After a short delay, the world’s first giga dive coaster opens on July 9 in the new Rancho de la Tormenta.
